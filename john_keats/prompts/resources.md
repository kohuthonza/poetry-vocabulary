# John Keats text resources

No specific reading edition has been established for the Keats `src.txt`. Use any edition or source identified in the user's initial processing prompt. If none is given, identify the edition or transcription of each full poem used for the approved typo audit and record its source URL, exact opening-verse identity and provenance in the temporary source manifest. A title alone is insufficient to identify a poem when titles repeat or differ across editions.

Download a usable full text for every approved poem before auditing its Words and relevant Visuals. Check the opening verse and neighboring lines, then compare every submitted entry in context. A search snippet, poem index, landing page or partial excerpt is insufficient. Keep the poem texts through vocabulary work and remove the temporary archive after verified completion, as required by the [common workflow](../../common/update_src.md) and [source helper guide](../../tools/README.md).

Treat differences in spelling, contractions, punctuation, lineation and titles between editions as possible variants. Do not silently use another transcription to correct the user's wording. When a source reading is consequential or the source edition is uncertain, show the evidence and ask for the user's typo decision. Use dictionaries for uncertain historical meanings, not as substitutes for poem text. If a one-line source header cannot be verified as the poem's opening verse, resolve the identity before processing it.

## Visual source research

For new Keats image sets, use the poem context to identify a selected plant, bird, mythic figure or historical object before selecting photographs or artwork. Check species names and object identities against the image's own source page; a Commons search label alone is not evidence. Wikimedia Commons File pages can supply official thumbnail URLs and artist/licence metadata through the shared [Commons helper](../../tools/README.md#approved-images). Review the file page and the resulting credit before download. The existing [Keats image index](../image_sources.md) identifies resolved subjects and aliases for reuse.
