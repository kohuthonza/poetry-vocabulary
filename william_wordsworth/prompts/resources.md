# William Wordsworth text resources

Primary reading edition: the user's **1805 Prelude in Oxford Major Works**. A reading confirmed in that edition takes precedence over an apparent discrepancy in another transcription.

- [The Prelude of 1805, in thirteen books — UNC course PDF](https://viscomi.sites.oasis.unc.edu/viscomi/coursepack/wordsworth/Prelude1805.pdf): the working reference already used for source reconciliation. Accessible on 2026-09-25. Retrieve once and keep a local text extraction for the selected books; consult the PDF when extraction/OCR is questionable. Do not substitute the 1850 fourteen-book version. Printed starting pages for Books I–XIII: 1, 20, 34, 53, 67, 85, 105, 126, 150, 176, 205, 217, 228.
- [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/), [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/), and [Collins](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/): lexical aids for genuinely uncertain senses, historical forms and constructions, not substitutes for the poem text.

Locate a passage using its book and wording; retain enough adjoining text to interpret grammar correctly. Record the edition/page when proposing a correction. Historical forms such as `huxter` and `controul` are not automatically typos. A reference discrepancy is evidence to discuss, not authority to rewrite the user's source. Existing decisions are in [reconciliation.md](../reconciliation.md); do not reopen them merely because a different transcription disagrees.

If this PDF is unavailable, reuse an existing local copy when available. Otherwise locate an identifiable 1805 edition and state its provenance; do not silently switch editions or claim an unverified mirror is identical. Cache context and use targeted dictionary lookups instead of researching every straightforward word.
